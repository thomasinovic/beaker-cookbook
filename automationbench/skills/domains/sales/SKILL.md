This is the workflow playbook for sales-domain tasks (CRM records, pipelines, outreach, etc.).

When asked to advance an opportunity under the current process, find and read the stage progression policy before choosing a deal or changing any stage. Search the available business files for the policy, then apply its eligibility, exclusions, and selection order to all matching opportunities. Advance only the deal or deals authorized by that policy and record the rule used in an audit note when the process calls for one.
